## 6. Risks of one-off implementations

- **Hook replication.** Any rollout page that adopts the `px-cycle-head`/`px-stage-item` markers — or copies the `:has()` pattern for new containers — inherits the page-enclosing frame (RC-01). Scope the selectors by the stable `st-key-*` classes once, centrally, before the first page slice.
- **Specificity-tie collapse.** Badge and notice colors win only through stylesheet injection order (ties at 0,2,1 and 0,1,1). A future edit to the global text rule or injection sequence silently re-inverts state colors, and the harness guards only the success badge — likely outcome: per-page `!important` patches. Raise the state selectors above the global rule (as KB-02 originally sketched) or extend the color assertion to every state and page.
- **Latent field-error bug invites local fixes.** When the Writing/Feedback slices notice their error messages lack the error voice (RC-04), the tempting move is a page-local color. Fix the single token (`--px-status-on-error`) in the shared layer first.
- **Undocumented variants get re-derived per page.** The sidebar selection recipe (`--px-status-info` tint + bold), the `.px-notice-limitation` variant (surface fill + 2px ink border, no accent bar — also below-fold/unverified), the 18px notice icon, and the mobile 2px bars are all implementation facts with no doc contract (RC-05..RC-09). Without freeze-time amendments, each page author will re-invent them slightly differently.
- **Separator improvisation.** Because `.px-divider`'s 4px-ink rule never renders (RC-10), pages needing rules may invent per-page hr styles. Either repair the rule or document the native hairline as the separator pattern.
- **Assertion-only below-fold components drift silently.** Until at least one full-scroll capture exists per page (RC-02), the focused panel, stage actions, and limitation notice can diverge on the other four pages without any artifact noticing.

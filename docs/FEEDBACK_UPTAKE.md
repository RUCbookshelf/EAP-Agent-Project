# Feedback-uptake candidates v0.5

An uptake candidate asks whether an observed revision is consistent with earlier structured guidance. It binds the
previous feedback/diagnosis, source and target submissions, local alignment evidence, a cautious status, confidence,
rule version and limitations.

Statuses are `supported`, `partially_supported`, `not_observed`, `contradictory` and `not_assessable`. They are not
causal conclusions. A major rewrite or incompatible analysis makes attribution especially weak and normally yields
`not_assessable`. Teacher review is required before any instructional interpretation.

Practice items separately record whether their content is a `student_source_sentence` or a
`synthetic_practice_sentence`, plus the generation version. The UI displays this source explicitly.

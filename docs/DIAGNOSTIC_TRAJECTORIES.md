# Diagnostic Trajectories v0.7

Primary evidence is a calibrated `selected_priority`. `eligible_diagnosis` is auxiliary. `monitored_signal`, `suppressed`, and `insufficient_evidence` remain research-only. Counts are stored separately.

Versioned working rules use two selected occurrences as emerging evidence, three as recurring evidence, and at least three comparable tasks plus two selected occurrences plus a current verified selected signal as a persistent pattern. A two-task recent absence window may support `recently_reduced_signal`. Mixed diagnosis versions are not silently combined.

Only a current selected and evidence-verified diagnosis can become a current learning target. Persistent, recurring, emerging and reduced are observation labels, not traits, mastery, improvement, decline, or causal effects.

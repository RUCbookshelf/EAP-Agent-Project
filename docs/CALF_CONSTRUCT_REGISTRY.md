# CALF Construct Registry v0.8

| Construct | Subconstructs | v0.8 status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lexical_complexity` | diversity, density, sophistication | `validation_pending` | Text features, not lexical ability or quality |
| `syntactic_complexity` | clausal, phrasal, coordination, dependency candidates | `validation_pending` | Candidate foundation only; no validated T-unit/clause score |
| `accuracy` | grammatical and lexical accuracy | `unavailable` | No automatic Accuracy measure |
| `product_fluency` | writing output rate | `prototype` | Production-condition proxy, not fluency ability |

The registry API rejects duplicate construct, metric-version, and unit-version registrations. Research activation requires formula, unit, minimum-data rule, fixture, reference, and limitations. Coherence, rhetorical organization, revision process, CEFR, and overall scoring are outside v0.8.

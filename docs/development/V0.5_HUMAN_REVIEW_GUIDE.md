# v0.5 human review guide

Review at least these cases against stored alignment and evidence IDs: light local editing, partial revision, major
rewrite, a persistent diagnosis, a diagnosis not currently observed, a newly observed diagnosis, supported and
partially supported uptake candidates, no observable relation to feedback, and incompatible analyzer versions.

For each case verify that source/target IDs and quoted text are correct; split/merge labels are plausible; no absence
is described as solved; no change is described as ability growth; uptake language is non-causal; exercise source is
accurate; major-rewrite and version limitations are visible; and one representative draft is used in long-term trends.

Record disagreements as researcher decisions. Do not silently tune thresholds from a single example.
